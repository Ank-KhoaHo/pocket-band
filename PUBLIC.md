# This repository is public

Which means the working material a maintainer keeps — design notes, decision records, a
backlog — is **not published here**. If you went looking for it, it is deliberately absent
rather than missing.

Nothing withheld is secret. It is working material for whoever develops pocket-band, and
someone using it has no need of it.

## What is published is what you need

- **[README.md](README.md)** — what pocket-band is and what it does
- **[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)** — how to build it, and what will get a change rejected
- **[ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md)** — direction, and the things deliberately not coming
- **[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)** — how to report something, and the documented limits that are
  not findings
- **[CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)** — what is expected of everyone here
- **[LICENSE](LICENSE)** — what you may do with this

The standing rule is that anything in the unpublished material which turns out to matter to
someone using pocket-band **gets repeated in a README or turned into a CI check** — and the
check is the better answer, because prose goes stale and a check does not.

## If something you need is not here

**Open an issue.** That is not a formality. A gap you can see is a gap worth reporting, and it
is the fastest way to get the published material corrected.
