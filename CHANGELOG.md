# Changelog

Every release of pocket-band, newest first, in the words of whoever uses it.

A released block's heading is the version, then the date: `## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-10`. The newest
released version is also what the `VERSION` file holds, and the name of a git tag (`v` followed
by the version).

## Unreleased

- The README says what pocket-band does not do, when to use something else, and answers the first questions.
- The player has an icon in the browser tab, and a shared link shows a picture of it.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-10

- **Play it online**, or download `pocket-band.html` and open it: the whole player in one file, no install.
- **10 cassettes:** folk, lo-fi, orchestra, chiptune, ambient, jazz café, synthwave, tavern, bossa and zen.
- **18 moods** in 5 groups, including **Rush** and **Chase** above 200 BPM.
- **Play modes:** Repeat, Through, All and Shuffle.
- **Layers:** Calm, Build and Tense: the loop split into parts, changing on the beat.
- **⤓ WAV:** download the loop you hear as a seamless 16-bit stereo file, ready for a game.
- **Mixtapes, ♥ favourites and My plays.**
